Portfolio

Wyndham Destinations

Pre-Production: Setting the Stage

With a deep understanding of their commitment to providing unforgettable vacation experiences, we crafted a detailed plan to showcase their various timeshare properties and exchange services. From scripting to location scouting, every aspect was meticulously planned to ensure we captured the essence of Wyndham Destinations' offerings.

On-Site Video Production: Capturing the Magic

With the pre-production phase complete, it was time to bring the destination to life on-site. Armed with our cameras and a passion for travel, we embarked on a journey to capture the beauty and allure of Wyndham Destinations' properties. From the sun-drenched beaches of Florida to the majestic mountains of the Rockies, we aimed to transport viewers to each destination, immersing them in the experience of vacation ownership. Through stunning visuals and captivating storytelling, we sought to convey the sense of adventure and relaxation that awaits guests at Wyndham Destinations.

Editing: Weaving the Narrative

Once the filming was complete, we transitioned to the editing room to craft the story of Wyndham Destinations. With meticulous attention to detail, we carefully selected footage, added music, and incorporated graphics to create a narrative that resonated with viewers. From highlighting the amenities of each property to showcasing the excitement of vacationing with loved ones, every frame was curated to evoke emotion and inspire travel.

Delivery to the Client: Sharing the Magic

The final step in the journey was delivering the finished video to our client, Wyndham Destinations. With anticipation and excitement, we shared the video, eager to hear their feedback. When the response came back overwhelmingly positive, it was a testament to the collaborative effort and dedication that went into creating the video. We were honored to have played a role in showcasing the beauty and excitement of Wyndham Destinations to the world.
