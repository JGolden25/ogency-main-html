Y-Cube Commercial

From pre-production to delivery, let's dive into the journey of creating a compelling video for this Texas-based company at the forefront of energy storage technology.

Pre-Production: Setting the Stage

Through in-depth discussions and research, we crafted a creative concept that would effectively showcase Younicos' energy storage systems and control software. From scripting to storyboard creation, every detail was meticulously planned to ensure we effectively communicated the company's innovation and vision.

On-Site Video Production: Capturing the Magic

Armed with our cameras and a passion for storytelling, we set out to capture the essence of the company's technology in action. From the sleek design of their energy storage systems to the intricate workings of their control software, we aimed to visually convey the sophistication and efficiency of Younicos' solutions. Through careful planning and coordination, we captured compelling footage that showcased the company's cutting-edge technology and its potential to revolutionize the energy sector.

Editing: Weaving the Narrative

Once the filming was complete, we transitioned to the editing room to weave together the footage into a dynamic narrative. With a keen eye for detail and a focus on storytelling, we carefully selected and arranged clips to highlight Younicos' key strengths and achievements. Through strategic editing techniques, music selection, and graphic overlays, we aimed to create a video that not only informed but also inspired viewers to learn more about the company and its offerings.

Delivery to the Client: Sharing the Magic

The final step in our journey was delivering the finished video to Younicos. With anticipation and excitement, we shared the video, eager to hear their feedback. When the response came back overwhelmingly positive, it was a testament to the collaborative effort and dedication that went into creating the video. We were proud to have effectively captured Younicos' vision of innovation and technology leadership and to have shared it with the world.

Project challenges

  • Location Constraints:Adapting to the unique environments where Younicos' energy storage systems were installed required creative filming techniques.
  • Date:1-3 June 2018
  • Services:Video Production, Editing/Post-Production, Overview, Products + Services
  • Client:Younicos
